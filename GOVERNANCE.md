# Governance

Agentic Evidence Lab is maintained by Ryuhmanov M under the Kizz organization.
The maintainer is the final integrator for releases, evidence-contract changes,
security decisions, and project scope during the alpha period.

## Decision principles

- Evidence must match the scope of the claim.
- Negative and inconclusive admissible results remain publishable.
- Maintainer-evaluated evidence is never relabeled as independent evidence.
- Capability projects such as Council retain separate repositories, versions,
  and release authority.
- A proposed schema or process change is not adopted until it is implemented,
  validated, and recorded in the owning artifact.

Material decisions should be proposed in an issue or pull request with the
question, alternatives, tradeoffs, and falsifier. The maintainer may accept,
reject, narrow, or defer a proposal and should record the rationale when it
changes a public contract.

This governance model is intentionally simple for the alpha. It may evolve as
repeat contributors and independent replicators appear.
