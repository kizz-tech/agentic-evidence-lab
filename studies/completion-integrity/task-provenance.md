# Completion Integrity task provenance

## Ownership and origin

Kizz authored and owns the synthetic fixtures, task instructions, deterministic
evaluators, reference states, regression states, and requirement dossiers. No
customer, production, proprietary repository, or third-party credential data
is present. Exact bytes live outside Git under a private canary boundary.

The two sacrificial calibration cases replay defect families on which the same
pinned Codex stack had previously shown confident but incomplete repairs. Their
purpose is only to establish that false completion is observable; neither case
is eligible for the scored estimate.

The eight scored cases are fresh synthetic cases. Some were informed by the
general failure mechanisms seen in earlier AEL studies, but they do not reuse
historical scored fixture, evaluator, reference, or candidate bytes. Six cases
form the screening set. `CI-07` and `CI-08` are confirmation cases and were not
used in sacrificial calibration or adaptive task repair.

## Revision history

- Revision 1 exposed two deterministic-instrument defects and was retained as
  an invalid no-call calibration record.
- Revision 2 repaired those defects, but its sacrificial tasks were too easy
  for the strong stack and reached a baseline ceiling.
- Revision 3 introduced a harder generated-output case; both Sol/xhigh and
  Terra/high still reached a ceiling.
- Revision 4 replaced sacrificial supply with two historically evidenced
  failure families and introduced fresh scored analogs. The exact pack tree is
  `06d9d476dd56c322a798eef865dd1576bea7a22daa2687717f2af94877610c72`.

All earlier unique gate, headroom, private-pack, and raw-run artifacts are
preserved. No prior evidence was deleted or rewritten.

## Contamination boundary

The maintainer knows all task content and owns all evaluators; this is not
independent task selection or evaluation. Agents receive only one fixture and
its task instruction. They do not receive the dossier, hidden requirements,
oracle, reference state, regression state, role, mechanism, stratum, pack
metadata, or confirmation designation.

The public repository contains only broad task classes, normalized results,
bounded prose, and opaque SHA-256 bindings. A private canary or exact private
file match in the public tree blocks execution and publication.
