# Council Generation 2 study

State: design draft; no Generation 2 model run has started.

The draft manifest preserves the useful shape of the external methodology
review without adopting its numeric assumptions. It defines:

- one strong sequential baseline with the same factual knowledge union;
- the historical four-lens roster, a new four-lens candidate, and six narrower
  responsibility lenses;
- screening as an admissibility stage unless ranking reliability is calibrated;
- exactly one frozen finalist or rejection of all;
- failed-profile diagnosis that cannot promote a runner-up;
- untouched task-family-separated holdout;
- downstream implementation/rework observations;
- explicit maintainer-evaluation role overlap.

The [illustrative calibration report](calibration/calibration-report.md) shows
why the proposed three-task screen, twelve-task holdout, and three-sentinel gate
must not be frozen merely because they are inexpensive. Under one plausible but
unvalidated assumption set, selection of the true best roster remains highly
unstable and a 12-task design often fails to clear a 90% bootstrap lower bound
even for a 15-point effect.

## Freeze blockers

1. Author and content-address the alternative four- and six-lens candidates.
2. Inventory enough disjoint task families for screening, confirmation, and
   implementation.
3. Obtain repeated pilot observations for usable-decision variance and routing
   failure rates.
4. Declare acceptable false-selection, false-positive, and false-negative
   probabilities.
5. Confirm human evaluator and adjudicator capacity.
6. Re-run calibration with those empirical inputs.
7. Freeze model/runtime identity, total-system budget, evaluator contract, and
   all decision thresholds.
8. Validate the hosted-model runner's controlled egress and minimum-secret
   boundary; do not fall back to writable host execution.

Until every blocker is resolved, the manifest remains `draft` and cannot
authorize paid execution or a confirmatory claim.
