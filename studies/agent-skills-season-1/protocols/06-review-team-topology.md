# 06 — One reviewer versus a review team

State: protocol draft; raw skill activation calibration completed; full
upstream multi-agent-topology conformance and effectiveness remain unrun.

## Decision question

At a matched total-system token and time envelope, does the exact pinned Every
`ce-code-review` topology produce more actionable, non-duplicate findings than
one strong accountable reviewer?

## Conditions and task design

- `B0`: one strong reviewer with the union of factual review guidance.
- `T1`: exact upstream topology only where the runtime supports it unchanged.
- `T2`: any Codex port is a separately named compatibility adaptation with its
  own source, topology, and prompt hashes.

Tasks span correctness, data integrity, concurrency, security, compatibility,
and safe negative controls. Budget counts every dispatcher, reviewer,
deduplication, integration, and retry call—not only the final answer.

## Measurements and decision

Primary: actionable defect recall after deduplication. Secondary: precision,
critical misses, duplicate rate, contradiction rate, integration loss,
generated work, cost, and latency. A team wins only within the frozen budget
and strata; more reviewers is not itself evidence of better review.
