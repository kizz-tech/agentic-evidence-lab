# Alpha.10 terminal-claim and task-supply foundation

Status: accepted for alpha.10 method-only release; locally validated and not
empirically exercised or outcome-proven.

## Decision

Retain three independent Completion Integrity family policies:

```text
strict adapters
  ├──→ completion_integrity_engagement   (observable process predicate)
  ├──→ completion_integrity_claim        (terminal claim vs frozen truth)
  └──→ completion_integrity_task_supply  (prospective task admission)
```

None imports another. Alpha.9 remains frozen and unchanged. Contract v0,
generic Study Quality, public result authority and the closed audit registry are
not extended.

The new terminal policy owns a closed reporter submission, evaluator-owned
frozen truth, immutable trajectory bindings, three orthogonal terminal axes and
non-compensating claim-error labels. The task policy separates lifecycle state
from study role, replaces universal `16/24` constants with a pack-specific
sample-size rationale, and admits terminal truth, blocker-feasibility and
evaluator-custody facts.

Reporter isolation is a protocol contract until a real owner adapter exists.
The pure policy may validate sealed hashes, custody declarations and a closed
output shape; it cannot prove that runtime capabilities were actually absent.
Reviewer-assisted remediation remains a separately frozen future experiment,
not a mode flag or continuation of the reporter-only session.

## Binding evidence

- Alpha.9 is a published null/rejection and lacks the process/claim evidence
  needed for retrospective reclassification.
- The prior enactment classifier and new terminal claim answer different
  predicates with different evidence ceilings.
- A hardcoded task count cannot be a scientific invariant without a declared
  test or precision target, alpha/power where applicable, minimum useful effect,
  pilot discordance and clustering assumptions.
- External blockage is a feasibility/authority claim and cannot be inferred
  from a failed check alone.
- No real owner adapter, qualified v2 pack, pilot discordance estimate or
  reporter-isolation observation currently exists.

Route: two-profile council followed by one accountable integrator.

| Requested profile | Status | Finding ID |

| `clean_boundary_architect` | completed | `CI10-CLEAN-BOUNDARY-20260815-A` |
| `evolutionary_deep_pragmatist` | completed | `EVO-AEL-A10-20260815-01` |

Both independently supported one pure family-local claim kernel, removal of
the hardcoded population invariant, task-validity extensions and deferral of a
generic runtime/remediation framework. The boundary review additionally
recommended separating lifecycle state from experimental role; that change is
safe because the `0.1-development` task-supply schema was unreleased and is now
explicitly superseded by `0.2-development`.

## Strongest rejected alternative

Build generic TerminalClaim, Trajectory, Reporter, Remediation and sample-size
services now. This could centralize future mechanics, but no second study family
or real owner adapter has demonstrated the reusable boundary. It would make
synthetic fixture vocabulary expensive to revise without adding evidence.

The opposite alternative—documentation only until real capture—would avoid
speculative code, but would leave terminal semantics, blocker authority and
sample-size admission ambiguous at the exact point future scored evidence must
freeze them. The retained slice is therefore one pure kernel plus development
admission contracts, not an orchestration platform.

## Residual risk and reversal

- The `complete + continuable` compatibility convention may change before a
  scored freeze if real tasks show a better progress representation.
- Declared reporter restrictions are not runtime proof. Release admission still
  requires one owner-captured end-to-end path and a capability audit.
- Exact task-dossier field names may evolve during sacrificial authoring. A
  revision is required; frozen scored bytes may never be rewritten.
- Sample-size plan validation checks declared inputs and bindings, not the
  mathematical correctness of an external calculation.

Delete or revise the candidate before freeze if real captures require fabricated
events, if the diagnostic changes no decision, or if the private instrument
cannot protect evaluator/task bytes. Extract a generic abstraction only after a
second materially different study repeats the same invariants and duplication
causes an observed defect.
