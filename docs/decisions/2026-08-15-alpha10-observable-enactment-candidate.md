# Alpha.10 releases an observable-chain instrument, not an empirical result

- Date: 2026-08-15
- Status: accepted for method-only release; empirically unexercised
- Scope: Completion Integrity family-local instrumentation

## Decision

Publish a small alpha.10 method instrument that classifies normalized evidence
into four non-compensating facets. Package publication does not promote
synthetic fixture conformance into empirical progress.

Alpha.9 correctly rejected the exact appended prompt policy because it changed
neither false completion nor accepted final state on its frozen task
population. Missing process evidence did not invalidate that decision. It
prevents a different claim: whether the intervention was observably enacted
and where its declared chain failed.

The candidate contains only:

- a pure Completion Integrity classifier;
- a strict file adapter that binds real policy bytes and exact input hashes;
- four non-compensating facets;
- repair-loop-compatible stage observations;
- synthetic known-state fixtures, including an honest alpha.9
  `not_assessable` case.

Normalized event labels remain caller-provided facts until a future owner
adapter captures real harness events. The term `observable_chain_complete`
therefore describes classifier input, not demonstrated real-world enactment.

## Boundary choice

Study Quality owns prospective design declarations shared across study
families. Contract v0 owns five stable public evidence objects. The new ledger
and event semantics currently belong to one intervention family. Promoting
them now would create a generic contract before repeated use establishes common
meaning.

The candidate leaves every published schema and alpha.9-bound artifact
unchanged. No public audit adapter is registered before real evidence exists.

## Task expansion

The owner preference to increase task count is compatible with the method, but
only after the next real task packs exist. The adopted development contract is
[Completion Integrity task supply](../completion-integrity-task-supply.md):
author more candidates than the scored target, require non-compensating oracle,
alternative-solution, semantic-mutant, environment, lineage, qualification and
privacy gates, then freeze fresh screening and untouched confirmation
separately.

The private inventory may use 16–24 only as authoring capacity. It is not a
powered minimum or target. Admission now requires a pack-specific hash-bound
power or precision rationale; `pending_pilot` blocks scored freeze. Repeats,
paraphrases, ports, calibration cases and weak roots do not increase
independent `n`.

The first draft encoded 16 synthetic task rows. Final audit rejected that as
count theatre: it created the appearance of scale without real tasks. The
replacement checker reports only bound private candidates and refuses to call
a pack admission-ready when its quality-passing independent roots fall below
the declared minimum.

## Review integration

Two independent Engineering Council lenses converged on the same boundary:

- `A10-CLEAN-BOUNDARY-01` supported a family-local pure policy and warned that
  unbound projections and mechanical release checks could overstate status;
- `AEL-EDP-ALPHA10-20260815-01` required the smallest classifier, an alpha.9
  `not_assessable` state, and no task-count theatre.

The final audit additionally found that the first implementation overclaimed
synthetic events, rejected legitimate repair loops, and conflated incomplete
and blocked terminals. The retained design binds policy and ledger bytes,
accepts repair loops, gives blockers explicit evidence semantics, and labels
synthetic events honestly.

## Admission and reversal

The instrument may be released because it repairs a named measurement defect
and its claim ceiling is explicit. It is not empirically admitted until a real
owner adapter captures the relevant facts and one of these produces an
admissible result:

1. downstream outcome closure;
2. genuinely role-separated reproduction;
3. separately authorized, frozen prospective study.

Revisit or remove the instrument if structured capture dominates behavior,
cannot avoid leaking private task content, fails known-state calibration, does
not change interpretation or action, or another study family reveals a better
shared boundary.
